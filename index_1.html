01 / About

From software at JPMorgan to growth at Got Sole.

Leading Business Development and Growth at Got Sole, the platform that grew from a sneaker convention into The World's Greatest Culture Festival, bringing sneakers, trading cards, streetwear, vintage, and luxury together under one roof in 14 cities.

With a background in computer science and previous experience building AI-powered intelligence products end to end at JPMorgan Chase (2024 - 2026). Utilizing that experience to help build the automated workflows growing Got Sole at scale.

That background shapes the whole approach. Beyond the deals, the job is owning a product and its full lifecycle: building the workflows, automations, and tech infrastructure that let a lean team scale a festival platform city after city. The business side of that equation was sharpened at Harvard Business School's Summer Venture in Management Program.
